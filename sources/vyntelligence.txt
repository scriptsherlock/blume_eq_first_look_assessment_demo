TITLE: Transforming Field Operations Through Video Intelligence: Why We Partnered with Vyntelligence | Blume Equity
FETCHED: 2026-11-16

The global infrastructure crisis extends far beyond ageing pipes and power lines. Across industries managing distributed assets, from telecommunications networks to retail operations, companies face an unprecedented convergence of challenges. The skilled trades workforce is experiencing its most severe shortage in decades. According to industry data, for every 5 experienced field technicians retiring, only 2 new workers enter the profession. This 60% replacement gap creates a knowledge crisis that coincides with escalating maintenance demands driven by extreme weather events and aging infrastructure, creating operational pressures that traditional approaches cannot address as industries need to find ways to “do more with less”.

The financial implications are profound. McKinsey estimates that service organizations could automate up to 70% of field service tasks through AI-powered workflows, yet most industries remain anchored to paper-based processes and desktop-era management systems. These legacy approaches create cascading inefficiencies: unnecessary trips into the field cost great amounts to utilities and telecoms annually, while compliance failures and quality issues compound these losses. Beyond the financial waste, these inefficiencies carry significant environmental costs. Vyntelligence’s deployments have demonstrated that reducing field visits by 50% saves approximately 4 million carbon miles annually, while faster detection and response to issues like water leaks have already conserved millions of gallons of water. Field visits that could be eliminated, rework that could be prevented, and fines that could be avoided represent massive addressable inefficiencies, where operational optimization directly translates to reduced carbon footprint and resource conservation.

The fundamental challenge lies in capturing and leveraging field knowledge. Every day, millions of field workers possess invaluable expertise about asset conditions, safety risks, and operational realities. This knowledge evaporates the moment they leave a job site. Meanwhile, an aging workforce takes decades of accumulated wisdom into retirement, leaving organizations scrambling to maintain service quality with inexperienced crews. Traditional documentation methods fail to bridge this knowledge gap, creating a vicious cycle where substantial portions of field work require rework or repeat visits. Compounding this problem, much of what happens in the field remains invisible to records: work quality, safety compliance, and actual time spent on tasks are either incorrectly documented or not captured at all. Vyntelligence addresses both challenges, capturing field knowledge before it disappears, while providing real-time visibility into field operations, enabling organizations to improve worker productivity, validate work quality, and identify process improvements that would otherwise remain hidden in the gap between field reality and office records.

Vyntelligence has built a platform that transforms how organisations capture, analyse, and act upon field intelligence. Rather than treating video as mere documentation, Vyntelligence converts brief field recordings into structured data and automated workflows. Their platform enables any worker to capture critical field moments that become actionable intelligence. Through AI models trained on millions of transactions and millions of real-world scenarios across years of operations, Vyntelligence doesn’t just record work; it understands it, validates it, and orchestrates the complex workflows that follow. This translated into one of the main UK utilities using Vyntelligence to automatically detect permits, compliance and safety risks and achieve an 85% fines reduction and eliminate audits, or another UK energy company reducing visits by 57% thanks to customers choosing video survey over physical visits.

What attracted us to Vyntelligence extends beyond their technology platform. This is a business that exemplifies the investment profile we seek: deep vertical expertise built through years of serving industry leaders, proven AI capabilities, and the operational maturity to serve mission-critical infrastructure. Already trusted by leading utilities, telecommunications providers, and retail operations across multiple markets, Vyntelligence has created a new category of enterprise software where video becomes the universal interface between field reality and operational intelligence.

This investment reinforces Blume’s conviction that transformative solutions in traditional industries come from companies that have earned their stripes over time. Vyntelligence exemplifies what we look for: businesses that have spent years building deep vertical knowledge before layering in AI capabilities, accumulating the operational credibility that utilities, telecoms, and infrastructure operators require from their technology partners. This combination of domain expertise, proven technology, and established trust takes time to develop but creates defensible positions in markets that matter.
