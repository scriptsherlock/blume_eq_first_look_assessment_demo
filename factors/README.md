Reference tables the Modeller may cite. Guardrails check emission factors and exchange rates against these rows.

| File | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| `uk_ghg_2025_vehicles.csv` | UK Government GHG Conversion Factors for Company Reporting 2025, flat file. Cars, vans and HGVs, kg CO2e per km and per mile. https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/greenhouse-gas-reporting-conversion-factors-2025 | Open Government Licence v3.0 |
| `defra_spend_sic_2023.csv` | Spend-based emissions multipliers by SIC code, UK 2023, current prices, excluding VAT. DEFRA / University of Leeds, published 30 June 2026. https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/uks-carbon-footprint | Open Government Licence v3.0 |
| `fx_ecb_snapshot.json` | ECB euro foreign exchange reference rates. Refreshed on each live run; this snapshot is used offline and in tests. https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/eurofxref/eurofxref-daily.xml | ECB reference rates, published for information |

DEFRA notes that spend-based multipliers estimate average full supply-chain emissions and that activity-based data should be used where available. They suit a first-look estimate, not a reported footprint.
